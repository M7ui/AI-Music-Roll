# Jazz Improvisation Guide

> **Focus:** Swing feel, complex harmonies, and creative improvisation.

---

## Core Jazz Concepts

### Swing Feel
- Eighth notes played as long-short (0.75 + 0.25)
- Triplet feel underneath
- Relaxed yet energetic

### Blue Notes
- Flattened 3rd, 5th, and 7th
- Gives that characteristic jazz sound
- Used sparingly for effect

---

## Jazz Chord Progressions

### Essential Progressions
1. **ii-V-I:** Dm7-G7-Cmaj7 (backbone of jazz)
2. **I-viio-ii-V:** C-Bdim-Dm7-G7
3. **Blues:** I7-IV7-I7-I7-IV7-IV7-I7-I7
4. **Rhythm Changes:** Based on "I Got Rhythm"
5. **Coltrane Changes:** iii-vi-ii-V with chromatic movement

### Extended Chords
- 7ths, 9ths, 11ths, 13ths
- Altered tones (b9, #9, b5, #5)
- Slash chords for voice leading

---

## Improvisation Techniques

### Scales for Soloing
- **Major scale:** Over major chords
- **Dorian:** Over minor 7th chords
- **Mixolydian:** Over dominant 7th chords
- **Locrian:** Over half-diminished chords
- **Whole-tone:** Over augmented chords
- **Diminished:** Over diminished chords

### Approaches
- **Target notes:** Hit chord tones on beats 1 and 3
- **Chromatic passing tones:** Connect chord tones
- **Enclosure:** Approach target from above and below
- **Arpeggios:** Outline the chord structure

---

## Jazz Melody Characteristics

### Range
- Wider than pop (2-3 octaves)
- More jumps and leaps
- Use of upper register for excitement

### Phrasing
- Syncopation is key
- "Call and response" with band
- Space and breath control

---

## Swing Rhythm

### Drum Pattern
- Ride cymbal: Quarter notes with swing feel
- Hi-hat: On beats 2 and 4
- Snare: Off-beat emphasis
- Bass drum: Walking pattern

### Bassline
- Walking bass: Quarter notes
- Chromatic passing tones
- Emphasize chord tones on strong beats

## Performance Tips

### Listening
- Transcribe solos from masters
- Study Charlie Parker, Miles Davis, John Coltrane

### Practice
- Learn tunes in all 12 keys
- Practice with a metronome
- Jam with others regularly
