# Pop Music Composition Guide

> **Focus:** Catchy melodies, simple harmonies, and danceable rhythms.

---

## Core Characteristics

### Key Features of Pop Music
- **Simple structure:** Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
- **Repetition:** Hooks and choruses repeat for memorability
- **Tempo:** 100-130 BPM (danceable)
- **Clear melody:** Singable, often in mid-range (C4-A4)

---

## Common Pop Chord Progressions

### Top 5 Pop Progressions
1. **I-V-vi-IV:** C-G-Am-F (most used)
2. **I-vi-IV-V:** C-Am-F-G
3. **vi-IV-I-V:** Am-F-C-G
4. **I-bVII-IV-I:** C-Bb-F-C
5. **ii-V-I:** Dm7-G7-Cmaj7 (jazz-pop)

### Four-Chord Song Examples
- "Let It Be" - The Beatles (C-G-Am-F)
- "Someone Like You" - Adele (G-D-Em-C)
- "Hallelujah" - Leonard Cohen (Am-F-C-G)

---

## Melody Guidelines

### Range
- **Lead vocal:** C4 (60) to A4 (69) - most comfortable singing range
- **Harmony:** 3rds and 5ths above/below melody

### Contour
- Gentle arcs (not too jumpy)
- Stepwise motion with occasional leaps
- Resolution on tonic at phrase ends

---

## Rhythm Patterns

### Drum Groove
- Kick: beats 1, 2, 3, 4
- Snare: beats 2, 4
- Hi-hat: 8th notes

### Bassline
- Root notes on beats 1 and 3
- Walking bass on off-beats

---

## Production Tips

### Instrumentation
- Piano/Keyboard: Chords
- Guitar: Strumming
- Bass: Root notes
- Drums: Standard pop beat
- Synth: Pad or lead

### Effects
- Reverb: Medium room
- Delay: Slapback or dotted
- Compression: Glue the mix

---

## Song Structure Template

```
Intro: 4 bars (instrumental)
Verse 1: 8 bars (storytelling)
Pre-Chorus: 4 bars (build)
Chorus: 8 bars (catchy hook)
Verse 2: 8 bars (more story)
Pre-Chorus: 4 bars
Chorus: 8 bars
Bridge: 8 bars (new section)
Chorus: 8 bars (final)
Outro: 4 bars (fade)
```

---

## Writing Checklist

✅ Melody stays in singable range  
✅ Chorus is higher than verse  
✅ Clear hook/repetition  
✅ Simple chord progression  
✅ Danceable rhythm  
✅ Emotional lyrics  
✅ Professional production
