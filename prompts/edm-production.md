# EDM Production Guide

> **Focus:** Electronic dance music with driving beats and powerful drops.

---

## EDM Structure

### Standard Structure
```
Intro: 8-16 bars
Build-up: 8-16 bars
Drop: 16 bars
Breakdown: 8-16 bars
Build-up 2: 8-16 bars
Drop 2: 16 bars
Outro: 8-16 bars
```

### Key Sections
- **Intro:** Sparse, sets mood
- **Build-up:** Layer elements, increase tension
- **Drop:** Full energy, main melody
- **Breakdown:** Contrast, prepare for next drop

---

## Common EDM Genres

### Characteristics by Genre
| Genre | BPM | Key Features |
|-------|-----|--------------|
| House | 120-130 | Four-on-the-floor, piano riffs |
| Techno | 125-140 | Minimal, repetitive, hypnotic |
| Trance | 130-140 | Builds, epic melodies, euphoria |
| Dubstep | 70-80 | Heavy bass, wubs, syncopation |
| Drum & Bass | 170-180 | Fast breaks, heavy basslines |
| Future Bass | 100-110 | Chord progressions, vocal chops |

## Drum Programming

### 4/4 Kick Pattern
```
Kick: 1, 1&, 2, 2&, 3, 3&, 4, 4&
Snare: 2, 4
Hi-hat: 8th notes (16th for faster genres)
Clap: 2, 4 (with reverb)
```

### Variation Techniques
- Ghost notes between main beats
- Roll fills before drops
- Sidechain compression on everything except kick

---

## Bass Design

### Types of Bass
- **Sub bass:** Low frequencies (80Hz and below)
- **Mid bass:** Body and character
- **High bass:** Attack and definition

### Bass Patterns
- **Root note:** Simple, on beat 1
- **Stepwise:** Moving between chord tones
- **Syncopated:** Off-beat emphasis

---

## Melody & Chords

### Chord Progressions
- Simple progressions (2-4 chords)
- Repetitive for danceability
- Use of suspended chords for tension

### Lead Sounds
- Synthesizers (saw, square, sine)
- Effects: Reverb, delay, chorus
- Pitch bends and portamento

---

## Sound Design Tips

### Synthesis
- Subtractivesynthesis for bass
- FM synthesis for leads
- Wavetable synthesis for textures

### Effects Chain
1. Compressor
2. EQ
3. Reverb/Delay
4. Limiter (on master)

### Sidechain Compression
- Duck bass when kick hits
- Creates pumping effect
- Use fast attack, medium release
